    How to use the tarot box

    There are many diffrent types of readings that you perform with tarot cards. While thissite is set up to teach users how to perform 5 main readings, you can perform others and use this page as a logbook.

    To begin, hover over the type of reading you would like to do from the list below, and place that many cards on the box. Make sure to them ordered, left to right!

    Daily Reading

    1 card

    This is a great read to do every day. The individual card gives you guidance in response to a question about the day ahead.

    Situation / Challenge

    2 card

    This is a quick and easy spread to anwser dilemmas. One card represents the situation and the other the challenge / obstacle.

    Past / Present / Future

    3 card

    A very common spread, it gives insight on a situation/question with each card relating to the past, present and future.

    Decision Making

    4 card

    A spread good for when you are stuck at a crossroads and you want guidance from the cards. This spread will help you weigh up your options.

    5 Card Cross

    5 card

    This spread is great for general guidance on an issue. It will help explore the problem and expose potential hidden unknowns.
